## Direction
Premium, restrained, editorial and investigative. Avoid cheap visual conventions.

## Prohibited without explicit project approval
- Green as primary brand color
- Electric blue
- Neon effects
- Hacker/cyberpunk clichés
- Aggressive gradients
- Dynamic Material You color substitution
- Unrequested redesigns
