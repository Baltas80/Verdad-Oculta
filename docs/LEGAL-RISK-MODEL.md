# Verdad Oculta — Legal Risk Model

## Status

Design and review baseline. This document identifies legal-risk areas; it is not legal advice and does not authorize publication or operation as a regulated reporting channel.

## Operating principle

Verdad Oculta is an independent disclosure and archive platform. It does not assume that a submission is true or false and does not require a predetermined interpretation of UAP, NHI, anomalous phenomena, secret programs, technology, testimony, or other subjects.

The platform must distinguish receipt, preservation, review and publication. A submission is not automatically a publication.

## Primary legal-risk areas

### Personal data and re-identification

Received material may contain personal data even when the contributor is anonymous. Public release must use a sanitized derivative and must consider direct and indirect identification. The project must document its privacy basis, retention, access controls, processor relationships, international transfers where relevant, and incident response.

### Confidentiality and secrets

Some material may be subject to statutory confidentiality, professional secrecy, contractual duties, classified-information rules, trade secrets, or other restrictions. The platform must not assume that possession by a contributor creates an unrestricted right to publish.

### Defamation, honor and reputation

Claims about identifiable people or organizations can create legal exposure. Publication should distinguish original allegations, documents, corroboration, analysis and established facts. Identifying third parties should be avoided unless there is a documented and reviewed reason to retain the identification.

### Intellectual property

Submissions can contain copyrighted documents, photographs, recordings, databases or other protected works. Publication requires a documented legal basis or an applicable exception, licence, permission, or legal review as appropriate.

### Safety and unlawful acquisition

The platform should not facilitate hacking, theft, interception, credential theft, unauthorized access, or other unlawful acquisition of material. The submission interface receives material already in the contributor's possession; it must not instruct contributors how to obtain it unlawfully.

### Minors and highly sensitive material

Material involving minors, health information, intimate material, precise locations, biometric information, credentials, or other highly sensitive content requires enhanced review and may require non-public handling or rejection.

### Platform and intermediary responsibilities

Before production, counsel must assess the legal entity, jurisdiction, hosting arrangements, content moderation/removal obligations, notice handling, applicable EU/Spanish law, and any cross-border exposure.

## Spanish/EU legal review gate

Before production, obtain written legal advice covering at minimum:

1. Appropriate legal entity and governance.
2. RGPD/LOPDGDD role and lawful basis for receiving, retaining and publishing material.
3. Data protection impact assessment requirements.
4. Anonymous submission architecture and source-protection commitments.
5. Applicability or non-applicability of Ley 2/2023 to the project's activities.
6. Defamation/honor and publication-risk procedures.
7. Confidentiality, trade-secret and classified-information issues.
8. Copyright and related rights.
9. Notice, complaint, correction and removal procedures.
10. Hosting, payment, tax and consumer obligations for memberships/donations.
11. International hosting, transfers and jurisdictional exposure.
12. Law-enforcement request handling and disclosure procedures.

## No absolute anonymity promise

The platform must not promise impossible guarantees. It should describe concrete technical and organizational protections and their limits. Endpoint compromise, legal orders, third-party compromise, operational mistakes, or future re-identification techniques can create residual risk.

## Publication gate

No publication of sensitive material is permitted until the publication-redaction pipeline, access controls, secure storage, logging controls, incident response and legal policy have been implemented and tested.

## Membership boundary

Membership never purchases a confidential submission, source identity, identifying metadata, privileged communications, or editorial control. Member-only material must be sanitized and explicitly approved for that audience.

## Evidence preservation

Where lawful and necessary, protected originals should be preserved separately from publication derivatives. Hashes, chain-of-custody metadata and processing manifests should be maintained without unnecessarily exposing source identity or sensitive metadata.

## Review status

This document remains a gate until reviewed by qualified Spanish/EU counsel before production. It should be updated when the legal entity, hosting model, payment provider, data flows or publication workflow changes.
