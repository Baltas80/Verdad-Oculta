# Verdad Oculta — Platform Mission

## Definition

Verdad Oculta is an independent disclosure and archive platform whose purpose is to allow people to safely share information, files and anonymous testimony they believe should be known.

It is not defined by a governmental institution, a predetermined scientific conclusion, a belief system, or a requirement that every submission fit a predefined explanation.

## Core principle

> We do not tell people what to believe. We provide a protected place to reveal what they know and preserve what they share.

A submission may concern UAP, NHI, anomalous phenomena, secret files, technology, historical events, testimony, or another subject. The platform does not decide the nature of the phenomenon merely from the category selected by a contributor.

## Operational flow

`RECEIVE → PROTECT → PRESERVE → REVIEW → DISCLOSE → ARCHIVE`

- **RECEIVE:** accept supported files and testimony through the protected submission flow.
- **PROTECT:** minimize identity and metadata exposure and apply the approved security protocol.
- **PRESERVE:** retain the protected original and integrity information under controlled access.
- **REVIEW:** assess provenance, integrity and publication suitability without imposing a predetermined explanation.
- **DISCLOSE:** publish material when the platform has authorized its release.
- **ARCHIVE:** preserve published and selected restricted material under explicit access classifications.

## Independence

Verdad Oculta is not a government archive and does not require governmental endorsement for its existence or editorial direction. Public records may be used as sources, but the platform's archive is independently operated.

The platform must not be presented as an official whistleblower authority, government reporting channel, intelligence service or guaranteed legal shield. Legal applicability and protections depend on jurisdiction and circumstances.

## Source protection

- Anonymous submission is a core product requirement.
- Source identity must be technically separated from submitted content where identity is collected at all.
- Source identity must never be sold or disclosed because of membership status.
- Member access must never expose confidential source material.
- No absolute anonymity claim may be made: endpoint compromise, network-level threats, legal compulsion and other risks must be addressed explicitly in the threat model.

## Publication philosophy

Verdad Oculta preserves the distinction between:

- what the contributor says;
- what the submitted material contains;
- what can be authenticated or corroborated;
- what remains unexplained;
- what is an interpretation or hypothesis;
- and what Verdad Oculta chooses to publish.

The platform does not need to label a submission as true or false merely because it concerns an unusual subject. Uncertainty can remain part of the record.

## Membership boundary

The membership model is defined separately in `docs/MEMBERSHIP-MODEL.md`. Membership finances the infrastructure and may provide access to selected archive material. It never purchases source identity, confidential leaks or editorial control.

## Security boundary

No real sensitive submission, payment or member-only sensitive archive is considered production-ready until the security implementation gate, cryptographic protocol, key lifecycle, secure storage, transport, privacy review and independent security review requirements have been satisfied.
